# Kong Dental Surgery — Homepage Redesign

## Website Overview

Kong Dental Surgery is a Singapore-based dental practice established in 2010, operating five clinics across Choa Chu Kang, Yew Tee, Jurong East, Teck Whye, and Sunshine Place. The practice provides comprehensive and affordable dental care, including general dentistry, children's dentistry, wisdom tooth extraction, dental implants, orthodontics, and restorative treatments.

The redesigned homepage focuses on creating a welcoming, family-oriented digital experience that highlights accessible dental care, simplifies treatment discovery, and helps patients find suitable clinics and appointment options more conveniently.

## Main Improvements

### 1. Clearer Homepage Messaging and Patient Guidance

**Previous Issue:** The original homepage introduced the practice and its clinic locations but did not sufficiently connect visitors' dental concerns with relevant services and immediate next steps.

**Redesign Improvement:** Established a clearer homepage introduction emphasising accessible, family-focused dental care, supported by prominent treatment exploration and appointment actions. The updated content hierarchy guides visitors from understanding the practice to identifying suitable services and taking the next step.

### 2. Improved Patient-Centred Treatment Navigation

**Previous Issue:** Although the homepage presented a broad catalogue of dental treatments, the services could be organised more intuitively around common patient needs.

**Redesign Improvement:** Reorganised treatment discovery around five primary patient needs: routine dental care, children's dentistry, tooth pain, missing teeth, and orthodontics. This structured approach helps visitors identify relevant services more quickly and reduces unnecessary browsing through individual treatment categories.

### 3. Simplified Clinic Discovery and Appointment Access

**Previous Issue:** The practice's five clinic locations and telephone numbers were available, but the clinic-selection and booking journey lacked a sufficiently connected presentation, particularly for mobile visitors.

**Redesign Improvement:** Prioritised the five clinic locations through a clearer clinic-selection experience, displaying relevant location and contact information alongside prominent call and appointment actions. The improved mobile experience provides more direct pathways to choosing a nearby clinic and arranging dental care.

## Design Improvements

The homepage redesign focuses on several targeted visual and usability enhancements:

- **Stronger Visual Hierarchy:** Established a clearer progression from the main introduction to treatment options, clinic locations, and appointment actions.
- **Improved Service Organisation:** Grouped treatments around common dental concerns to make service discovery more intuitive.
- **Welcoming Visual Presentation:** Emphasised a clean, approachable, and family-oriented design consistent with the practice's values of kindness, service, and honesty.
- **Enhanced Trust Communication:** Increased the prominence of the dental team, patient-care information, and the practice's established history.
- **Accessible Clinic Navigation:** Organised five clinic locations into a clearer selection experience with direct contact options.
- **Mobile-Friendly User Experience:** Prioritised readable content, responsive layouts, and accessible booking and telephone actions.
- **Consistent Design Structure:** Improved spacing, typography, content alignment, and visual consistency throughout the homepage.

## Summary

The redesigned Kong Dental Surgery homepage addresses three primary usability and communication challenges: unclear first-screen guidance, insufficiently patient-centred treatment organisation, and fragmented clinic-selection and appointment pathways.

The improvements establish a more informative, welcoming, and accessible patient journey while reinforcing the practice's positioning around affordable, convenient, and comprehensive dental care for individuals and families across Singapore.
